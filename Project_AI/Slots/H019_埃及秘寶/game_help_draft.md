## HOW TO WIN

| 简中 | English |
| --- | --- |
| 盘面为6轮5行。相同普通符号与百搭符号合计达到8个或以上即可中奖。 | THE GAME IS PLAYED ON A 6-REEL, 5-ROW GRID. A WIN IS FORMED WHEN 8 OR MORE MATCHING REGULAR SYMBOLS AND WILD SYMBOLS APPEAR. |
| 同一盘面可同时形成多个符号奖项。 | MULTIPLE SYMBOL WINS MAY BE AWARDED ON THE SAME GRID. |

## CASCADING FEATURE

| 简中 | English |
| --- | --- |
| 中奖符号会被移除，其余符号向下掉落，新符号补满空位后再次结算，直到没有新中奖。 | WINNING SYMBOLS ARE REMOVED. REMAINING SYMBOLS CASCADE DOWN AND NEW SYMBOLS FILL THE EMPTY POSITIONS. WINS ARE EVALUATED AGAIN UNTIL NO NEW WIN IS FORMED. |

## WILD AND C2 MULTIPLIER

| 简中 | English |
| --- | --- |
| 百搭符号可替代所有普通符号，但不可替代C1或C2。只有参与中奖消除的百搭符号会转换成C2。 | WILD SUBSTITUTES FOR ALL REGULAR SYMBOLS, BUT NOT C1 OR C2. ONLY WILD SYMBOLS PARTICIPATING IN A WIN CONVERT INTO C2. |
| 最终盘面上的所有C2倍数相加，并乘上该次旋转的消除总赢分；没有C2时倍数为1。 | ALL C2 VALUES ON THE FINAL GRID ARE ADDED TOGETHER AND APPLIED TO THE TOTAL CASCADE WIN OF THAT SPIN. IF NO C2 IS PRESENT, THE MULTIPLIER IS 1. |
| C2可能显示2、3、4、5、8、10、12、15、20、25、50、100、150、200、250、500或1000倍。 | C2 MAY REVEAL 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X, 8X, 10X, 12X, 15X, 20X, 25X, 50X, 100X, 150X, 200X, 250X, 500X, OR 1000X. |

## FREE GAME

| 简中 | English |
| --- | --- |
| 基础游戏最终盘面出现4个或以上C1可获得15次免费旋转。 | 4 OR MORE C1 SYMBOLS ON THE FINAL BASE GAME GRID AWARD 15 FREE SPINS. |
| 免费游戏最终盘面出现3个或以上C1可增加5次免费旋转，单场上限为50次。 | 3 OR MORE C1 SYMBOLS ON A FINAL FREE GAME GRID AWARD 5 ADDITIONAL FREE SPINS, UP TO 50 FREE SPINS PER FEATURE. |
| 免费游戏中的C2倍数会累积至该场免费游戏结束。 | C2 MULTIPLIERS ACCUMULATE UNTIL THE FREE GAME ENDS. |

## FEATURE PURCHASES

| 简中 | English |
| --- | --- |
| 购买特色价格为总投注的100倍，并直接触发免费游戏。 | BUY FEATURE COSTS 100X THE TOTAL BET AND DIRECTLY TRIGGERS THE FREE GAME. |
| 超级特色价格为总投注的500倍，并使用超级免费游戏轮带；C2最小为10倍。 | SUPER FEATURE COSTS 500X THE TOTAL BET AND USES THE SUPER FREE GAME REELS; C2 VALUES START AT 10X. |
